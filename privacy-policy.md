## 1. Information Collection and Use

### A. On-Device Diagnostic Testing (No Personal Data Collection)
Phone Hardware Test & Checker performs all hardware, sensor, screen, battery, and audio diagnostic tests **100% locally on your mobile device**. 
* We **do not** collect, store, record, or transmit your personal diagnostic test results, hardware telemetry, photos, audio recordings, files, or personal identifiers to external servers operated by Ouayat Studio.

### B. Third-Party Advertising and Analytics Services
To keep this application free of charge, Phone Hardware Test & Checker uses third-party advertising and consent services:

1. **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)**

   * AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, advertising identifiers (`AD_ID`), IP addresses, coarse location data, and app interaction data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
   * **AdMob Privacy Policy:** [https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)

2. **Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK**
   * Used to present European Economic Area (EEA) and UK users with consent forms for personalized or non-personalized advertising under GDPR and ePrivacy Directive.
   * Users can manage or reset their consent preferences anytime in the app under **Settings > Ad & Privacy Preferences**.

## 2. Device Permissions and Purpose

Phone Hardware Test & Checker requests access to specific device features solely to conduct hardware diagnostic tests:

* **`android.permission.VIBRATE`**: Enables the app to test the device's haptic vibration motor.
* **`android.permission.BLUETOOTH` & `BLUETOOTH_CONNECT`**: Allows the app to verify whether the Bluetooth adapter hardware is enabled.
* **`android.permission.ACCESS_WIFI_STATE`**: Allows the app to check whether Wi-Fi hardware is present and active.
* **`android.permission.INTERNET` & `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`**: Allows the app to initialize AdMob advertising services and display consent forms.
* **`com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID`**: Allows the Google Mobile Ads SDK to access the Google Advertising ID for ad frequency capping and fraud prevention.
